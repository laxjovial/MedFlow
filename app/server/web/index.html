Patient records that stay yours — even offline

MedFlow keeps your clinic's records on your own computers, where no one else can touch them. It works with no internet at all, and it's simple enough that you don't need an IT team. Want an off-site safety copy? MedFlow can place one, fully scrambled, in a cloud folder you own — we never hold your data and never bill you for storing it.

Why clinics choose MedFlow

Built on what makes medical software actually get used

⚡

Type a few letters of a name, a phone number, or the patient card number — they're on your screen right away. Writing up a visit is one page, not five pop-up boxes.

One calm colour, clear headings, and tables you can actually read. The software stays quiet and out of the way, so staff can think about patients instead of fighting the screen.

Grows with your facility

Maternity, Outpatients, Pharmacy, Theatre — you make the list. Add, rename, or remove departments any time as your clinic grows. Nothing is fixed in advance.

File every patient where they belong

Pick a patient's department on their record. See at a glance how many patients each department holds.

When the worst happens

Two safety nets, both under your control

💾

A copy you can hold

One click makes a backup file. Keep it on a flash drive in a drawer. It's a single file — you can email it to yourself if you like.

☁️

A copy far away, in your cloud

If fire or flood takes the whole building, the flash drive goes with it. So MedFlow can also send an encrypted copy to a cloud folder you own — Google Drive-style WebDAV or any S3-compatible bucket. It's scrambled with a secret phrase only your clinic knows: the cloud company stores unreadable data and nobody but you can open it. MedFlow never sees your records and never charges you per month for storage.

How it works

Create your account

Sign up with a password or Google. Your first account leads the facility (it can add colleagues and settings); each new member gets a role you choose — doctor, nurse, front desk and more.

Register patients and chart visits

Demographics, vitals, diagnoses, medications, allergies, labs and notes — each with validation, timestamps, and a running clinical timeline.

Run the front desk

Schedule appointments and walk them through check-in, in-progress, completed or no-show. The dashboard shows today at a glance.

Own your data forever

One-click backups, CSV/JSON exports, printable charts. When you add staff, share read-only access with temporary scoped visitors.

Built for the places other software forgets

Rural clinics. Crisis response. Areas where the internet comes and goes. MedFlow keeps every record safely on the machines in front of you — and when a connection does appear, it can catch up on its own. No internet, no problem. Internet, better.
