Security & privacy

MedFlow's threat model starts at your front door, not a data center you can't inspect.

🔑

Passwords

Argon2id hashing — never plaintext, never reversible. Bcrypt hashes remain readable for migration. Minimum 8 characters enforced at creation.

🎫

Sessions

HMAC-signed bearer tokens with expiry, secret stored per-installation beside the database. Permissions resolve live per request — deactivation and expiry take effect instantly.

👤

Google sign-in

Optional. ID tokens are verified against Google's published keys with audience, issuer, and expiry checks; the email comes from the verified profile, never self-reported. Disable it by simply not configuring a client ID.

⏳

Temporary access

Guest accounts expire on a clock you set and are limited to the exact patients you pick. Expired accounts are refused at login and cut off mid-session. Revocation is one click.

📜

Audit trail

Sign-ins, record changes, exports, transfers and syncs are recorded with actor, device, and timestamp — filterable in the app, exportable as a dataset.

🏠

Data locality

No telemetry, no license servers, no third-party calls. The database is a plain SQLite file on your disk; backups and exports are ordinary files.
